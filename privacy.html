Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

HollowFrame AutoPoster is a private creator publishing tool operated by Hollow Frame Studio (taufiqhippy57@gmail.com) to publish original animated short films directly to authorized creator accounts.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize HollowFrame AutoPoster via TikTok Login Kit, we only receive the OAuth access token and basic account identifiers (such as display name and open ID) strictly necessary to upload and publish video content on your behalf.

2. How We Use Information

Access tokens are stored locally on the creator's private workstation and are used solely to invoke TikTok's Content Posting API upon explicit creator approval. We do not sell, rent, or share user data or tokens with any third party.

3. Data Retention & Revocation

You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok account security settings or by deleting local configuration files. For questions, contact taufiqhippy57@gmail.com.
